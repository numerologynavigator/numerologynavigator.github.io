DebitRadar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19-October-2026

DebitRadar helps you see your recurring auto-debits (UPI AutoPay, NACH/e-mandates, card subscriptions) and alerts you before money is debited. Short version: your SMS and your debit data stay on your phone. We run no servers that receive them and we do not sell your data.

1. SMS access and why we ask for it

With your permission, DebitRadar reads your bank SMS on your device only, to recognise recurring auto-debit messages (for example "Rs 649 debited for Netflix via UPI AutoPay"). From these it builds your list of subscriptions and mandates and works out when the next debit is due.

All SMS processing happens on your phone. Your messages are not uploaded to any server, not shared with us or any third party, and are never used for advertising. We only look at messages that describe a recurring bank debit; ordinary personal messages are ignored.

2. What is stored, and where

The merchant name, amount, dates and category of each detected debit are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. A short snippet of the matching SMS may be kept locally so you can see the history of a debit. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

3. What we do NOT access

No bank login. No account numbers or OTPs are collected. No contacts, no location, no call logs. The app has no account or sign-in.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions. This is separate from your SMS, which AdMob never receives. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. Premium users see no ads.

5. Purchases

Premium (a one-time unlock or a yearly plan) is sold through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; the app only learns whether you are a premium user.

6. Notifications

Pre-debit and price-hike alerts are created on your device from the data above. No notification data is sent anywhere.

8. Deleting your data

Delete any debit inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. You can also revoke SMS permission anytime in Android Settings → Apps → DebitRadar → Permissions.

9. Children

DebitRadar is intended for adults managing their own finances and is not directed at children under 13.
